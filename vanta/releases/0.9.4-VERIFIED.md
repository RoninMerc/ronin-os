## Implemented changes

- ChatProtocol stops at supported provider-confirmed terminal events rather than waiting unnecessarily for the connection to close. Early EOF, truncation, malformed output and unknown completion reasons remain incomplete.
- ForgeTransportRecovery adds explicitly approved, checkpointed recovery of unfinished code-inference requests. At most two automatic connection retries per task, delayed 10 and 30 seconds. Existing request, model-change, recovery-action and compiler-attempt limits are retained.
- Existing paused tasks have Recover connection & resume -> Approve & resume. New task preferences expose Recover dropped connections. Approval discloses that an interrupted provider request may already be charged. Existing jobs are not silently opted into paid replay.
- User/Android cancellation, checkpoint-write failure, TLS trust failures, HTTP rejections, policy refusals, media generation and compiler submissions do not enter generic transport replay.
- Compiler-reported source/test files and corresponding production classes are prioritised in repair context. Test guidance preserves read-only LiveData interfaces and meaningful assertions rather than exposing mutable state or deleting tests.
- Eligible short polling for an already submitted Android build remains in a finite foreground continuation (normal 15-second polling, at most 20 minutes from submission). Longer waits retain persisted Android scheduling.
- Encrypted diagnostic records include network stage, status, received bytes, elapsed/idle timing, exception/cancellation source and sanitised request ID, without credentials or request/response bodies in transport metadata.
- No storage schema change. Existing core provider, storage, model, voice and signing implementations retained. Only Vanta Android and its own verification workflows changed; no external worker or unrelated product was modified.
